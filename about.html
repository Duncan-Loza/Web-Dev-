About Clear Blue Plumbing

Getting to know our local plumbing business

Who We Are

Clear Blue Plumbing is a local plumbing business based in Witpoortjie, Roodepoort. The business serves customers in Roodepoort and the surrounding areas, including homeowners, tenants, landlords, property managers, and small businesses.

Clear Blue Plumbing provides plumbing assistance for a range of residential and business plumbing needs. Services include general plumbing repairs, leak repairs, blocked drains, toilet repairs, tap repairs, pipe repairs, maintenance, and emergency plumbing assistance.

Our Approach

The business aims to provide a straightforward and reliable plumbing service for customers who need help with common plumbing problems. Clear Blue Plumbing focuses on clear communication and helping customers understand the plumbing service they require.

Whether the problem involves a leaking tap, blocked drain, damaged pipe, or another plumbing issue, customers can use the website to learn more about the available services and make an enquiry.

Who We Serve

Clear Blue Plumbing is intended to serve customers throughout Witpoortjie and surrounding areas of Roodepoort.
